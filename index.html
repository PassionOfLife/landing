A little about us

Fresh perspective.
Serious care.

PassionOfLife is a creative studio led by students and young people. We help local businesses get the quality online presence they deserve without paying big agency prices.

We work with cafés, restaurants, independent shops, and other small businesses to create polished, easy-to-use websites that make a great first impression and help customers find you. Thoughtful work, straightforward communication, and fair pricing are at the heart of what we do.

Young people lead the work from the first conversation to the final details. We keep things personal and work with budgets that suit smaller businesses.

Who is behind PassionOfLife?

PassionOfLife is led by students and young people. We bring different interests and perspectives together to create thoughtful work with our project partners.

What kinds of projects do you take on?

We focus on affordable, high quality websites for local businesses, including cafés, restaurants, independent shops, and local services. We can also help with visual identities and other digital ideas. If you’re unsure whether your project fits, tell us about it.

How much does a project cost?

We believe a professional website should be within reach for small businesses. Pricing depends on what you need, so we’ll discuss a clear scope and fair price before any work begins. There’s no obligation to move forward.

Do I need a fully formed idea?

Not at all. A rough sketch, a problem you want to solve, or a simple “what if?” is a great place to start.
